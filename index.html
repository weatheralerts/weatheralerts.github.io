Frost and Freeze Alerts for Gardeners

A single cold night can wipe out tender seedlings, tomatoes and potted plants. Weather Alerts lets you set a low-temperature threshold for your location and sends a notification when the forecast drops below it, so you have time to cover plants, bring pots inside or drain outdoor taps.

How to use it

  • Save your garden or allotment location.
  • Set a temperature threshold, for example 2°C / 36°F, as an early frost warning.
  • Get an alert before the cold night arrives.
