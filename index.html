Why families use a custom weather alert app

Most weather apps expect you to remember to open them. Busy households rarely have that luxury. Between the school run, work, activities and visits to relatives, it is easy to miss the one hour of the day when a downpour, a sudden cold snap or a thunderstorm is due. Weather Alerts flips the routine around: instead of you checking the forecast, the app keeps an eye on it and lets you know when something you care about is expected.

The idea is simple and is described in detail on the official Weather Alerts website: you choose locations, you choose the weather conditions that matter, you set your own thresholds and timing, and the app notifies you. Alongside your own rules, it can also show official severe weather warnings from supported national meteorological services, with the issuing agency credited.

For parents this means fewer surprises. A notification the evening before that freezing conditions are likely at the school address is far more useful than discovering an icy pavement at 8 a.m. with two children and a scooter.

Set it up once, in three steps

  1. Save the places that matter. Add home, the school or nursery, a grandparent's town, the sports ground or a holiday rental. Locations can be found by search or by GPS.
  2. Choose conditions and thresholds. Pick the weather type, such as rain, temperature, wind gusts or UV index, set the level that matters to you and decide when you want to hear about it.
  3. Get notified. Receive a notification when the forecast matches your rule, immediately or up to seven days before the expected event, or when an official warning is issued.

Each saved location keeps its own set of alerts, so a rain rule for the school and a heat rule for the grandparents' garden never get mixed up.

Alerts that suit family life

Rain and snow

Hear when precipitation is expected to start or stop and set thresholds for light, moderate or heavy rain and snow, including sleet, freezing rain and drizzle. Perfect for deciding on wellies or a lift by car.

Heat and cold

Monitor heat, cold, freezing conditions, heat index and sudden temperature spikes, so you know when to plan shade, water bottles or an extra layer.

Wind and gusts

Track wind speed and wind gusts before trampolines, garden furniture or a cycle to school become a problem.

Thunderstorms and lightning

Weather description alerts can flag lightning, hail and thunderstorms, and depending on your region you can receive official severe thunderstorm warnings.

UV index

Set a UV level that works for your family and get a nudge to pack sun cream and hats before a sunny day out.

Air quality

Watch AQI and PM2.5 thresholds, useful on days when pollution or smoke may be affecting the air around your home.

Official severe weather warnings, in one place

Your own rules are only half of the story. Weather Alerts can also deliver official severe weather warnings from supported national meteorological services. Depending on where you live, that can include tornado, severe thunderstorm, flash flood, hurricane, winter storm and blizzard warnings. Each one keeps its official agency attribution, so you know exactly who issued it.

You can also control how notifications reach you. Custom sounds, Quiet Hours and Active Hours let you mute non-urgent alerts during bedtime or the school concert, while critical warnings are still allowed through. That balance matters when the phone is also the family alarm clock.

A typical week with Weather Alerts

On Sunday evening you glance at the widget and see an active alert: heavy rain is expected on Tuesday afternoon at the school address, right at pick-up time. You arrange a lift in advance. On Thursday a heat index rule for the grandparents' town fires two days ahead, so you call to remind them to keep the curtains closed and drink water. On Saturday a gust alert for the football ground arrives the night before, and the match kit goes in the car with an extra jacket. None of it required opening a forecast.

More than notifications

Weather Alerts is also a complete forecast app. You get hourly and daily forecasts with temperature, feels-like, precipitation probability, wind, humidity, visibility, UV and air quality for your current and saved locations. Interactive radar maps help you follow rain, snow and storms as they move, and home-screen widgets show current conditions, official warnings and active custom alerts without opening the app.

Weather data comes from Apple Weather (WeatherKit), which draws on national and international meteorological services. The app is available on Android through Google Play.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set different alerts for home and my child's school?

Yes. You can save multiple locations and create separate weather conditions and thresholds for each one.

How far ahead will I be warned?

You choose. Alerts can arrive immediately when conditions match or up to seven days before the expected event.

Does the app show official government weather warnings?

Yes. Weather Alerts can provide official severe weather warnings from supported national meteorological services, with agency attribution. The warnings available depend on your region.

Can I stop alerts waking the kids at night?

Yes. Quiet Hours and Active Hours control when non-urgent notifications are delivered, while critical warnings can still come through.

Is there an alert for strong sun?

Yes. You can set a UV index threshold and be notified when it is expected to be reached.

Do I need Premium for notifications?

Push notifications for custom weather alerts are a Premium feature with a seven-day free trial. Official severe weather warnings remain available alongside your custom alerts.

Where does the weather data come from?

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit), using information from national and international meteorological services.
