Rain Alerts With a Custom Threshold You Actually Control

A chance-of-rain percentage is not a plan. Twenty percent might soak a picnic or leave the pavement dry, and a generic shower icon rarely says which outcome you should prepare for. What you need is a rain notification app that watches the places you care about and pings you when rainfall crosses a limit you chose. Weather Alerts for Android is built for that: custom rain alerts tied to your locations, with a custom rain threshold you set yourself so light drizzle and a washout are not treated the same.

Why chance-of-rain icons fail outdoor plans

Outdoor events, school runs, gardening windows, and travel packing all hinge on how much rain arrives—not on a vague regional hint. Checking the forecast by hand still leaves gaps between looks. An Android rain alert flips the habit: you name home, work, a venue, or a garden, define what “too wet” means for that place, and get notice early enough to act—move the start time, cover seedlings, pack a jacket, or postpone a delivery.

Set the rain line that matches each place

One threshold rarely fits every pin on your map. A market stall may need a stricter rain alert than a short walk to the office. In Weather Alerts you save several places and attach rain rules that match each one. When the forecast for that spot crosses your custom rain threshold, the notification arrives while you still have room to rearrange the day.

Useful rain-alert setups include:

  • Outdoor events and markets that need a dry setup window
  • Commutes where a heavy shower changes bike or walking gear
  • Gardening days when light mist is fine but a soaking is not
  • Travel packing so jackets and covers land in the bag in time
  • Family visits when you want the same rain watch on another town

You stay in charge of sensitivity. Quiet when drizzle is harmless; loud when the amount matters.

Lead time beats last-minute panic

A rain alert that arrives after you are already outside is mostly regret. Weather Alerts aims to notify early enough to act: postpone, reroute, pack differently, or check on someone elsewhere before the pavement turns slick. That is the difference between a rain notification app and another forecast tab you forget to open.
